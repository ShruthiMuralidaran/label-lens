How it works

From tiny print to a clear, personal explanation.

The same label raises different questions for different people. LabelLens keeps your reason visible at every step.

1

Set your profile

Record your allergens, known skin triggers, or ingredients you want to watch. Categorise each as an allergy, trigger, or goal so the results are grouped by severity.

2

Snap or upload a label

Take a photo of the ingredient panel on any packaged food, personal care, or household product. For nutrition values, include the nutrition facts table too.

3

Get a personal breakdown

See which words matched and why, grouped by severity. If the label is unclear or cut off, LabelLens tells you what it could not check.

Why it is personal

More than a generic ingredient lookup.

A peanut match, a fragrance trigger, and a goal to reduce preservatives should not look like the same alert. LabelLens keeps the reason attached to every finding.

  • Findings point to the exact words found on the label.
  • Nutrition values come from the printed panel, not guesses.
  • Unclear or missing information stays visible, not hidden.
"The ingredients are the same. What matters on that label is personal."

How it works

From tiny print to a useful explanation.

The same ingredient can matter for different reasons. LabelLens keeps the reason visible, from a known allergy to a personal shopping goal.

01

Set your lens

Record an allergen, a known trigger or an ingredient you prefer to reduce. Keep each reason distinct.

02

Read the label

Provide the ingredient panel from food, personal care or household products. Food nutrition values come from the nutrition panel.

03

Review the evidence

See relevant words highlighted with a plain-language reason. An incomplete or unclear label calls for another look.

Built around your context

More than a generic ingredient dictionary.

A peanut match, a fragrance trigger, and a goal to reduce preservatives should not look like the same alert. LabelLens explains what was found and why it relates to your own profile.

  • Ingredient findings point to the words supplied on the label.
  • Food nutrition uses values actually printed on the pack.
  • Unclear or missing information stays visible.

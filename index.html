Why it is personal

More than a generic ingredient lookup.

A peanut match, a fragrance trigger, and a goal to reduce preservatives should not look like the same alert. LabelLens keeps the reason attached to every finding.

  • Findings point to the exact words found on the label.
  • Nutrition values come from the printed panel, not guesses.
  • Unclear or missing information stays visible, not hidden.
"The ingredients are the same. What matters on that label is personal."
